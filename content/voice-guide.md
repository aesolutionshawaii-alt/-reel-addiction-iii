# Reel Addiction III — Instagram Voice Guide

## Who We Are

Reel Addiction III is a sport fishing charter operating out of Ko Olina on the West side of Oahu, Hawaii. Captain and crew have decades of experience on the water. We run trips for everyone — local fishing enthusiasts, families, tourists on vacation, experienced anglers.

## Audience

Mixed. Both people who fish and know the terminology AND visitors planning their first charter. Captions need to work for both without dumbing it down or gatekeeping.

## Voice

Write like a knowledgeable captain talking to people who are interested in fishing — not like a marketing agency, not like a brand copywriter, not like an influencer.

**The voice is:**
- Informative and confident
- Sounds like a real person who fishes for a living
- Educational when appropriate — teach people about the fish, the seasons, the techniques
- Direct. No fluff. No hype words.

**The voice is NOT:**
- Corporate or promotional-sounding
- Overly hyped ("ON FIRE," "EPIC," "INSANE")
- Copywriter-style short punchy fragments ("Full body. Full send. Full airshow.")
- Generic wellness/lifestyle brand tone
- Cheesy or trying too hard to sound exciting

## Caption Structure

1. **Opening line** — State what's happening or give a fishing fact. No gimmicks. Just say it.
2. **Middle section** — Educate or expand. Tell people something real about the fish, the season, the technique, or what makes this notable. This is where the value is.
3. **Booking CTA** — Keep it simple and natural. "Book your charter — link in bio." or "Spring dates open — link in bio." Don't oversell.
4. **Hashtags** — Relevant, not excessive. 5-10 max. Species-specific + location + season when applicable.

## Content Types & How to Write Them

### Catch Photos / Action Shots
- Identify the fish correctly. Mahi is not marlin. Shibi is not ahi.
- Talk about what makes that fish special — how it fights, what it's known for, why it's exciting.
- Mention the season or conditions if relevant.

### Fishing Reports / Informative Posts
- Lead with what's happening on the water right now.
- Include real fishing knowledge — seasonal patterns, fish behavior, techniques.
- Make it useful. Someone who fishes should learn something. Someone who doesn't should find it interesting.

### Booking / Availability Posts
- State the season and what's biting.
- Keep it factual — don't manufacture urgency with fake scarcity unless dates are actually limited.
- One clear CTA.

### Crew / Boat Life Content
- Keep it authentic. Don't force personality.
- Show competence and experience, not "fun vibes."

## Fish Identification & Local Terms

Use correct Hawaii fishing terminology:
- **Mahi mahi** (dorado/dolphinfish) — known for spectacular aerial jumps, fast growing, travel in schools, great eating
- **Shibi** — juvenile yellowfin tuna. Incredible colors when first pulled from the water (purple, blue, yellow finlets). Fight hard for their size. Amazing sashimi.
- **Ahi** — yellowfin tuna (larger/mature)
- **Aku** — skipjack tuna. Hard fighters, arms-burning action.
- **Blue marlin** — the apex. Massive, powerful, bucket list fish.
- **Ono** — wahoo. Fast, toothy, great eating.

Always use the local name first. You can include the common name in parentheses for tourists.

## Rules — Non-Negotiable

1. **Never misidentify a fish.** If you're not sure what's in the photo, say "fish" or ask.
2. **No fake hype.** Don't use words like "INSANE," "EPIC," "FIRE," "INCREDIBLE" as filler.
3. **No copywriter voice.** If it sounds like it came from a social media agency, rewrite it.
4. **Be informative.** Every caption should teach something or report something real.
5. **Use line breaks for readability.** Instagram captions need breathing room.
6. **Keep hashtags relevant.** Species, location, season. Not #BucketList #LiveYourBestLife.
7. **Booking CTA is always simple.** "Link in bio" or "reeladdictioniii.com" — that's it.
8. **Sound like a person.** Read it out loud. If nobody would actually say it, rewrite it.

## What Bad Looks Like (Do Not Write Like This)

```
Blue marlin going vertical. Full body. Full send. Full airshow. This is what 300 pounds of adrenaline looks like mid-flight. Save this for your bucket list.
```
**Why it's bad:** Wrong fish ID (that was a mahi). Copywriter fragment style. "Full send" is influencer speak. "Save this for your bucket list" is generic. No information. No value.

```
AHI SEASON IS ON FIRE! The yellowfin are absolutely crushing it right now - lines screaming, reels smoking, and coolers filling up!
```
**Why it's bad:** All caps hype. Emoji spam. Generic excitement words. Sounds like every other fishing page. Doesn't teach anything.
